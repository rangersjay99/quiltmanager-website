Quilt management made simpler

QuiltManager is being developed as a growing platform for managing the projects, inventory, events, and records that keep quilting organizations running. Our goal is to provide clear, practical tools without making technology overwhelming.

Planned Solutions

Individual tools that can grow with your organization.

Charity Quilt Management

Track quilts in storage, charity needs, distributions, upcoming commitments, and yearly accomplishments.

Quilt Show Management

Manage quilt entries, categories, check-in, judging, ribbons, display locations, tags, and claim tickets.

Personal Quilt Collections

Preserve information about completed quilts, makers, recipients, patterns, dates, photographs, and quilt history.

Fabric & Supply Tracking

Organize fabric, batting, backing, thread, tools, donated materials, and other quilting supplies.
